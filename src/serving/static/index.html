How to read this page
Fraud score
The model's estimate, from 0% to 100%, that a payment is fraud.
Cut-off
Payments scoring at or above the model's cut-off (shown on each payment) are flagged. It was chosen to balance missed fraud against false alarms.
Risk level
High: score of 90% or more. Medium: flagged, but below 90%. Low: below the cut-off, not flagged.
Fraud caught
Out of all real fraud in the held-out test data, the share the model flagged (recall).
Alerts that are real
Out of all flagged payments in the test data, the share that really were fraud (precision).
Hour
The simulation hour of the payment. PaySim covers 743 hours, about 31 days.
Amounts
In PaySim's currency units, as in the source data.
